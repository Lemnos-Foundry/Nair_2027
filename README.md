# Mosiria vs. Everyone: evidence trail

A sourced documentary outline and trail of evidence on Geoffrey Mosiria's fallouts with Nairobi Governor Johnson Sakaja, Babu Owino and Oga Obinna, and where Jalang'o fits. Compiled 24 Oct 2026 from public sources.

- `index.html`: the site (five-act outline with timestamped clips, claims-vs-evidence table, timeline, source register)
- `transcripts/`: YouTube auto-generated captions for every cited clip, each line linked to its timestamp
- `serve.sh`: serve locally on port 8999

Allegations are attributed to the person making them and are not findings of fact. Captions are machine-generated; check against the video before quoting. Video clips belong to their channels.
