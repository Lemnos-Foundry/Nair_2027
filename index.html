Mosiria vs. Everyone

Over three years Geoffrey Mosiria went from Governor Sakaja's most-praised chief officer to a fired rival running for his seat. Along the way he fell out with Babu Owino and Oga Obinna. This file traces each relationship through what the people involved said on camera and what the press reported, and keeps each man's claims separate from established fact.

The cast

"Mosiria vs friends" is mostly right. The exception is Jalang'o: we found no direct fight with Mosiria after 2025. His part in the story is as Babu's public backer.

Geoffrey Mosiria

Nairobi chief officer 2022 to Oct 2026 (Health, then Environment, then Public Participation); 2027 governor hopeful
Centre of the story. Built his profile on TikTok enforcement videos.

Johnson Sakaja

Governor of Nairobi
Appointed Mosiria, praised him, then removed him on 21 Oct 2026. Has said nothing on record.

Babu Owino

MP, Embakasi East; 2027 governor hopeful
Rival since University of Nairobi student politics. Mutual insults; Mosiria accuses him of taking a bribe, which Babu denies.

Oga Obinna

Comedian, YouTuber, talk-show host (Steve Thompson Magana)
Feud since Feb 2026. Rival defamation demands of KSh10M and KSh20M.

Jalang'o

MP, Lang'ata (Phelix Odiwuor)
Close to Babu. Says Babu "will be governor". Mosiria clashed with him once, in Apr 2025.

      Gaps & next steps

      • Sakaja's side. His office "declined to respond" to NTV. Try the governor's "Let's Talk" podcast (launched 3 Sep 2026) or a request to County Hall for comment.
      • Jalang'o in 2026. We found nothing from him on Mosiria's firing or bid. His original July 2025 interview with Mwakideu should be pulled from his own channel; here we use a re-upload.
      • Is Obinna on Babu's side? In his 21 Oct video Obinna seems to say "we" are going for that office with "Babu… 2027, August 11th." We found no other source for this. Treat it as a lead, not a finding.
      • Was there ever a friendship? We found no evidence that Mosiria was ever close to Obinna or Babu. Babu and Mosiria knew each other as rivals at UoN. The only "friendship" on record is Mosiria's own story of stepping aside for Babu in 2017.
      • Budget figures. The KSh1.2B to KSh100M health budget claim can be checked against Nairobi County budget estimates and Controller of Budget reports for FY2023/24 and FY2024/25.
      • Mpasho headline, 8 Oct 2026: "Mosiria warns Babu Owino over Oburu death claims." We couldn't open it or find it elsewhere. Unconfirmed.
